Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a crypto wallet to use Aurevon services?
No. We mint to a free email wallet for you.
What blockchain are Aurevon NFTs on?
Base (ERC-721), Coinbase's Ethereum L2: Ethereum-grade security at near-zero gas fees (~$0.01 per mint).
What happens if I cancel my subscription?
You keep your NFT. Your Discord role follows your active plan, so a cancelled monthly membership loses its role; resubscribe and it comes back.
Can I sell or transfer my NFT?
Your pass is held in an email wallet we create for you; transfers are handled on request.
How is this different from a regular membership?
It's on-chain, so anyone can verify you're an Aurevon holder, and it sets your Discord role automatically.
Is this a security or investment?
No. Aurevon NFTs are utility tokens — access passes to community features and service perks. They are not securities, do not represent equity in any company, and do not carry any expectation of profit. Any secondary market value is determined by open market dynamics, not by Aurevon.
What if I don't understand blockchain at all?
That's who Aurevon Labs is for. We handle the minting and the wallet; the blockchain part stays invisible unless you want to learn more.
What's the difference between the Community Member NFT and the underwriting NFTs?
The Community Member NFT ($29.99/mo or $349.99 lifetime) gives you full Discord access and networking — the same community that underwriting clients get — but without any underwriting services. If you also need underwriting, purchase an Aurevon Capital package instead — it includes both the community access and the underwriting deliverables.
